# Road Remains

**Road Remains** is an interactive civic education game set in the fictional Sri Lankan village of Alupotha, six weeks before an election. The project aims to improve voter knowledge, help users navigate complex registration processes, and counter election misinformation[cite: 1, 4].

## 🎮 The Premise
An election is coming. Alupotha is not ready. Neither are you.

As a resident of Alupotha, you are tasked with navigating the quiet pressure of a community making up its mind. You will visit the Grama Sevaka Office, check the Elections Commission notice board, and decide how to engage with the misinformation circulating in the village's social circles.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Civic Simulation:** Experience a realistic (though fictional) election timeline in the weeks leading up to the vote[cite: 1].
* **Information Health System:** Balance your own understanding of the electoral process against the spread of misinformation[cite: 4].
* **Character-Driven Stories:** Play as different residents—each with unique perks, challenges, and perspectives on the town[cite: 4].
* **Interactive Map:** Explore a living, isometric village to complete civic objectives and discover secrets[cite: 1].

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript[cite: 3, 4].
* **Design:** Adobe Creative Suite.
* **Localization:** Supports English, with Sinhala and Tamil foundations[cite: 4].
