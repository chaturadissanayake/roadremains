About the Project

Voting Matters is a civic education project developed to improve voter education and counter election-related misinformation in Sri Lanka. Developed with support from LIRNEasia. Available free in English, Sinhala, and Tamil. The game does not tell you who to vote for. That part is entirely yours. All characters and parties depicted are fictional.
