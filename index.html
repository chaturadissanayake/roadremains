About the Project

Tale of Alupota is a civic education project developed to improve voter education and counter election-related misinformation in Sri Lanka. Developed with support from LIRNEasia. Available free in English, Sinhala, and Tamil. The game does not tell you who to vote for. That part is entirely yours. All characters and parties depicted are fictional.

Atakatus
Age 19. First-time voter.
Just moved to Alupotha from Kurunegala. Doesn't know anyone yet. His uncle won't stop sending WhatsApp messages.
PERK Curiosity: At every location, Atakatus has one extra dialogue option no other character gets — he can ask why something works the way it does, and someone will actually explain it.
DISADVANTAGE Exposure: Uncle Sirisena is family. Ignoring his messages has a social cost that Imali and Kumaran do not face.

Imali
Age 34. Community leader.
Professional, confident, warm. She already knows how this town works — but the town is changing.
PERK Network: Imali has social capital everywhere in Alupotha. Queues move faster. People share information more readily. She occasionally gets warned about problems before they reach her.
DISADVANTAGE Assumption: The game will sometimes give Imali information she believes she already knows — but has slightly wrong. Correcting these assumptions is worth it. Ignoring them is not.

Kumaran
Age 28. Migrant worker from the North.
Tamil-speaking, navigating a bilingual bureaucracy in a town where most signs are in Sinhala. Every form is a small obstacle.
PERK Persistence: Every step Kumaran completes earns him more than the same step earns the others, because every step costs him more. He also unlocks content the other characters cannot reach.
DISADVANTAGE Distance: Kumaran does not have Atakatus's family network or Imali's community connections. Accurate information is harder to find, and misinformation is slightly harder to counter without official sources.
