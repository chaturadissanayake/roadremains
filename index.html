About

Road Remains is a civic education game set in the fictional Sri Lankan village of Alupotha, six weeks before an election. You play as a resident navigating voter registration, misinformation, and the quiet pressure of a community making up its mind.

The game does not tell you who to vote for. That part is entirely yours.

Legal All characters, candidates, and political parties depicted are fictional. Electoral procedures reflect Sri Lankan law as of 2024.
